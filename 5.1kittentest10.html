What “keeping it” actually involves (so you can decide) Newborn (few days old) orphaned kittens usually need: - **Feeding every 2–3 hours**, around the clock (including overnight), with kitten formula (not cow’s milk) - Stimulating to **pee and poop** after each feeding (they can’t do it alone yet) - Careful **temperature control** (they can’t regulate their body temp) - Monitoring for: - Weakness, refusal to eat - Cold body, gasping, pale gums - Diarrhea, bloating This is basically like having a newborn human baby, but more fragile, and with worse odds. If you tried to do this alone while already exhausted: - You’d probably have **chronic sleep deprivation** - High chance of emotional crash if the kitten doesn’t make it despite everything That doesn’t mean you *shouldn’t* do it—only that you should choose it with your eyes open, not from guilt. --- ## 3.

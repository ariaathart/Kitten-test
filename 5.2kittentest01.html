Put the kitten in a small box/carrier with soft towels. - Provide gentle heat: **heating pad on LOW under half the box** or a warm (not hot) water bottle wrapped in cloth. - Aim for *warm to the touch*, not sweaty/hot. - **Do not feed if the kitten feels cold**—a cold kitten can aspirate and crash. ### Feeding basics (only once warm) - Use **kitten milk replacer (KMR)**, not cow’s milk. - Feed belly-down (natural position), never on its back. -

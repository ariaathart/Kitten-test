If you choose to keep the kitten, here are a few things to expect and tips: ### Immediate Needs: - **Warmth:** Newborn kittens can’t regulate their body temperature. Keep it in a warm space using a heating pad (set to low), a warm water bottle wrapped in a towel, or even just your touch. - **Feeding:** They need kitten formula (not cow’s milk, which can be harmful) every 2-3 hours. A bottle or syringe is ideal. Ask a vet or pet store for kitten milk replacer. - **Stimulating to potty:** Kittens this young can’t go to the bathroom on their own; you’ll need to gently rub its backside with a warm, damp cloth after feeding. ### The Decision to Keep It: It's a lot of work, but support is out there.

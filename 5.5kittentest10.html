I’m really sorry — that’s a lot to be handed with no warning. And yes: caring for a kitten that young is **exhausting even for experienced fosters**. **You do not have to “keep it” to do the right thing.** The best option, if you’re already drained and inexperienced, is to **transfer it ASAP to a neonatal kitten foster/rescue** — someone who already has supplies and can feed every 2–3 hours. That may give it a better chance than you trying alone. ## What to do right now 1. **Call the vets back** and ask directly: “Do you know any neonatal kitten fosters, rescue bottle-feeders, vet techs, or shelters that take newborns?” 2. **Contact local rescues/shelters urgently** Search/post: - “neonatal kitten foster near me” - “bottle baby kitten rescue” - local cat rescue Facebook groups - humane society / animal control / SPCA Say: **“Newborn kitten, eyes closed, found alone, needs bottle-feeder immediately.”** 3. **Keep it warm — but don’t overheat it** Use a heating pad on low under **half** the box/towel, or a warm water bottle wrapped in cloth. It must be able to crawl away from heat. 4. **Do not feed if it’s cold** A cold newborn can’t digest and can aspirate. Warm first. 5. If you must feed temporarily: - Use **kitten milk replacer**, not cow’s milk. - Feed belly-down, never on its back. - Go slowly. - Newborns need feeding about **every 2–3 hours**, including overnight. - After feeding, gently stimulate pee/poop with warm damp cotton/tissue. ## Should you keep it? Honestly: **only if you can handle round-the-clock care for days to weeks**, or if you have someone to rotate shifts with you. If you’re already exhausted, the kindest choice may be to **find an experienced foster immediately**. That is not abandonment — that is giving it the best possible shot. And if both vets think survival odds are very low, also ask them plainly: **“Is the kitten suffering, and would humane euthanasia be kinder if no foster can take it?”** That’s a heartbreaking question, but sometimes it’s the compassionate one. For now: keep it warm, start calling/posting urgently, and don’t beat yourself up. You already did more than most people would.
